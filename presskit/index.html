Caiana Duo releases “Bebê” — a Baião tribute to Hermeto Pascoal

Released: September 18, 2026
Location: Munich, Germany

Brazilian instrumental duo Caiana Duo — Abdallah Harati on 7-string nylon guitar and João Araújo on pandeiro and percussion — has released Bebê, a Baião interpretation of a composition by the late Brazilian composer and multi-instrumentalist Hermeto Pascoal.

The arrangement was built around solo guitar, with percussion layered on top, and was tested live several times before it was recorded. The recording combines traditional Brazilian percussion with organic sound textures of forest, sea and birds. It carries Hermeto Pascoal's playful, genre-defying spirit through the rhythmic drive of Baião, a style rooted in Brazil's Northeast.

About Caiana Duo. Caiana Duo is a Brazilian instrumental project formed by Abdallah Harati (7-string guitar) and João Araújo (pandeiro). Their sound blends Choro, Samba, Brazilian jazz and other Afro-Brazilian rhythms in intimate acoustic performances, with original compositions and reworked pieces by Pixinguinha, João Pernambuco, Heitor Villa-Lobos and Baden Powell. The duo has performed at the International Choro Festival München, at a solidarity concert at Amerikahaus, and at Seidlvilla. About Abdallah Harati. Brazilian-born guitarist, composer and producer based in Munich, known for his 7-string nylon guitar playing in Choro, Samba, Afro-Brazilian music and Brazilian jazz. Bio

Musician, producer and business strategist Abdallah Harati is originally from São Paulo, Brazil, grew up in São José dos Campos, and since 2015 has been established in Munich, Germany. Prior to that, Abdallah lived in Guatemala City, Mexico City, San Juan, Dallas, Stockholm, Dubai, Hanoi and Nairobi.

Since 2020, Abdallah has released more than 25 singles and EPs, completely independently, across the major digital platforms, passing 1 million cumulative streams. His songs have been featured in outlets like All About Jazz, and added to the “Relaxing Spanish Guitar” and “Violão Brasileiro” Spotify editorial playlists.

Abdallah also produces other independent musicians and artists under the HaratiMedia label, and performs with Caiana Duo alongside percussionist João Araújo. He organizes the weekly Choro München roda de choro and gives masterclasses and workshops from time to time.

He started learning music in childhood with flute, piano, and finally guitar, completed classical guitar studies, graduated as an Electronic Engineer from the College in Bauru (São Paulo state, Brazil), and was awarded Best Solo Guitar performer at the USP Music Festival in 1994 and 1995. He was part of the Choro Club in São José dos Campos and the Escola Portátil de Música initiative led by Luciana Rabello, Maurício Carrilho, Pedro Amorim, Álvaro Carrilho and Celsinho Silva. He has studied at Berklee College of Music in Boston and at Carmen de Las Cuevas in Granada, Spain, and is currently pursuing a Master's in Music Business at Berklee Online.
