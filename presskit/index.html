Bio

Musician, producer and business strategist Abdallah Harati is originally from São Paulo, Brazil, grew up in São José dos Campos, and since 2015 has been established in Munich, Germany. Prior to that, Abdallah lived in Guatemala City, Mexico City, San Juan, Dallas, Stockholm, Dubai, Hanoi and Nairobi.

Since 2020, Abdallah has released a steady catalog of singles and EPs, completely independently, across the major digital platforms. His songs have been featured in outlets like All About Jazz, and added to the “Relaxing Spanish Guitar” and “Violão Brasileiro” Spotify editorial playlists.

Abdallah also produces other independent musicians and artists under the Harati Media label, and performs with Caiana Duo alongside percussionist João Araújo. Full story: see the Biography page.

He started learning music in childhood with flute, piano, and finally guitar, completed classical guitar studies, graduated as an Electronic Engineer from the College in Bauru (São Paulo state, Brazil), and was awarded Best Solo Guitar performer at the USP Music Festival in 1994 and 1995. He was part of the Choro Club in São José dos Campos and the Escola Portátil de Música initiative led by Luciana Rabello, Maurício Carrilho, Pedro Amorim, Álvaro Carrilho and Celsinho Silva. He has studied at Berklee College of Music in Boston and at Carmen de Las Cuevas in Granada, Spain, and is currently pursuing a Master's in Music Business at Berklee.
