Podcast

I'm a global citizen who happens to be in Munich, Germany, since 2015, after almost 20 years living in several countries across 4 continents. Music is perhaps the best way I express my soul and my heart. There are many musical stories, and I hope they entertain you.

I share stories from my home country, Brazil, and the strong influence of Choro, Seresta, and Samba that came from home. Then I travelled to several different places, always with my guitar — entertaining topics from Central America, particularly Guatemala and Mexico, my adventures in Spain, Sweden and continental Europe (with special attention to Germany, where I've lived since 2015), and interesting places in Africa and Asia, based in Kenya and Vietnam respectively.

I also feature a few interviews with interesting people, always music related.
